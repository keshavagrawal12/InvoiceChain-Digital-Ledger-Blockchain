Bank Portal

Approving a request checks the shared blockchain for the invoice fingerprint. Approved records wait in the pending list until you mine a block.

How a bank handles a request

  1. Approve runs the fraud check: the invoice's fingerprint is searched on the blockchain and in every bank's pending list. If any bank already has it → FRAUD ALERT.
  2. If it is new, the record goes to Pending records. Other banks can already see it, so they can't approve the same invoice.
  3. Mine Block writes the pending records permanently into a new block, which is copied to all three banks. Check it in the Blockchain Explorer.
