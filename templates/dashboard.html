An invoice can be financed once.

Small businesses borrow against unpaid invoices. The fraud is taking the same invoice to several banks. Each bank only sees its own books, so nobody notices.

InvoiceChain gives every bank one shared ledger. Before lending, a bank checks it for the invoice. If another bank got there first, the request is stamped duplicate and rejected.

How a loan is checked

  1. The supplier creates an invoice. Five of its details are hashed into a fingerprint.
  2. The supplier sends the invoice to a bank for a loan.
  3. The bank searches the shared ledger for that fingerprint. Found means fraud alert.
  4. If it is new, the bank mines a block: proof-of-work seals the loan into the ledger.
  5. The block goes to the other banks, which check it and add it to their copies.
